# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kiln to be a project where people can do good technical work without
having to spend energy on anything else. Everyone participating — contributors,
maintainers, and people opening their first issue — is entitled to a harassment-
free experience regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that builds the project:

- Critiquing the code rather than the person who wrote it.
- Assuming the other person is reasonable and has context you do not.
- Accepting feedback gracefully, and giving it specifically.
- Saying "I was wrong" early and without ceremony.
- Making room for people who are new to the project or to systems programming.

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, or dismissing people rather than arguments.

Disagreement is not a violation. Technical disagreement is how the project gets
better, and a strongly argued objection is welcome. Contempt is the line.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through GitHub's
report mechanism or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's safety comes before the reported person's
comfort.

Maintainers will respond proportionately. Depending on severity and pattern, that
may mean a private correction, a public warning, a temporary ban from project
spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good
faith may face the same consequences.
